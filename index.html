I am a computational chemist working on how enzymes recognize their substrates and how small molecules can be designed to interfere with that recognition. At Umeå University I work with the groups of Anna Linusson (Chemistry) and Yuri Schwartz (Molecular Biology), combining AI-based structure prediction with long-timescale molecular dynamics to study SET-domain lysine methyltransferases such as ASH1L.

I did my Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical Sciences (Computational Chemistry) at DPSRU and the Translational Health Science and Technology Institute (THSTI), India, with Prof. Shailendra Asthana. There I used enhanced sampling and free-energy methods to discover and explain allosteric inhibitors of the deubiquitinase USP7, and later led virtual screening campaigns against IRGM and CXCR3 that produced hits active in cells and animal models.

My work is applied and collaborative: most of my projects pair simulations with experimental partners in biochemistry, cell biology and infection biology. My postdoc in Sweden was funded by a Kempestiftelserna fellowship.

Substrate recognition by SET-domain methyltransferases

AlphaFold3-seeded microsecond MD of Ash1/ASH1L reveals an active conformation not seen in crystal structures, which I use to search for new non-histone substrates with protein–peptide modeling and experimental validation.

    Research

    Simulations that explain recognition, and use it to find molecules

    I treat AI structure prediction as a starting point, not an answer: predicted models seed long MD runs and enhanced sampling, which tell us which conformations are real, how ligands and substrates bind, and where to intervene.

    Themes

    Epigenetics

    Ash1/ASH1L and non-histone substrates

    ASH1L is a SET-domain lysine methyltransferase linked to development and disease. Starting from AlphaFold3 models, microsecond MD exposed a catalytically competent groove that crystal structures do not show. I use this state to rank candidate substrate peptides by docking and interface dynamics, and the best candidates go to the Schwartz lab for testing.

    • AlphaFold3
    • µs MD
    • HPEPDOCK
    • HDOCK
    • PIPER
    Parasite proteases

    Metacaspases of Trypanosoma brucei

    Structure prediction and ensemble simulations, checked against biological experiments, to work out the structure–function relationships of these cysteine proteases. Manuscript in preparation with Anna Linusson.

    • AlphaFold3
    • Boltz
    • Chai
    • MD ensembles
    Oncology · DUBs

    Allosteric checkpoints in USP7

    MD mapped druggable hotspots and cryptic pockets in the deubiquitinase USP7. Metadynamics, binding-pose metadynamics and umbrella sampling explained the (un)binding of known inhibitors and guided a structure-based workflow that found a new inhibitor, published in JCIM (2025).

    • Metadynamics
    • Umbrella sampling
    • MM/GBSA
    • Glide
    Infection · Inflammation

    Structure-based discovery with experimental partners

    Virtual screening and MD for targets in tuberculosis (ArgA, PPK1, proteasome), SARS-CoV-2 (RdRp, main protease), dengue (CXCR3) and chikungunya, plus SIRT1 and IRGM, several of which led to compounds with in vitro and in vivo activity and three patent applications.

    • Virtual screening
    • FEP+
    • Pharmacophores
    • QSAR

    Computing

    Large simulation campaigns run on Swedish national HPC through NAISS, on Kebnekaise (HPC2N) and Dardel (PDC), managed with SLURM and Python automation.
